# Sales agent guide

For order desk staff. You take orders by phone and from chat pages, confirm them with the
buyer and follow them to delivery. You see your own orders and the queue of orders nobody has
taken yet. You do not see your colleagues' orders.

To practise on the preview, see the testing guide,
[section 1](../testing-guide.md#1-orders-and-buyers). Sign-in help is in the
[README](README.md).

## Your screens

- **Orders**: **Order desk**, **Queue**, **All orders** (yours) and **Returns by reason**.
- **Customers**: the buyers of your orders.
- **Catalog** and **Stock**: look up a product and what is in stock.
- **Performance**: your own targets.

## Take an order

1. Open **Orders** and press **New order**, or open the **Order desk** tab.
2. **Phone**: type the buyer's mobile number (`01XXXXXXXXX`, `+880` or Bangla digits). For a
   returning buyer, their recent orders and saved addresses appear. **Use this address** fills
   a saved one.
3. **Buyer name**, then **Area (thana, district)**: type, then pick from the list with the
   arrow keys and Enter. Then type the **Address**.
4. The **Delivery zone** and its charge follow the area. If no zone covers it, type the charge
   by hand.
5. **Find a SKU**: type 3 or more characters of the code and pick the item. Set the quantity.
   Each line shows how many are **available**.
6. Choose the **Channel** (Facebook, WhatsApp, phone call and so on). Add **Notes** if needed.
7. A **Promo code** is optional. Its discount is worked out when you save.
8. Press **Save and confirm** (Ctrl+Enter) if the buyer has agreed, or **Save as pending** to
   confirm later.

If **Possible duplicate** appears, this number ordered the same item in the last 24 hours.
Check with the buyer before you save. Press `?` on the desk to see every keyboard shortcut.

## Your discount limit

You may price an order at most **20% below the catalogue** in total. Every unit price you
lower and the **Discount (৳)** you type count together. A promo code does not count toward
the 20%.

If an order needs more, the save is refused. Do not split the order or change prices to get
round the limit. Ask an org admin: they may give a bigger discount.

## Work the queue

1. Open **Orders** → **Queue**. It lists new orders from pages, websites and colleagues.
2. Press **Claim** on an order to make it yours. If someone claimed it first, the screen says
   so. Pick another.

## Move an order on

Open an order from **All orders**. Only the steps allowed for its status are shown.

- **Confirm**: the buyer agreed. The stock is now held for this order.
- **Hold**, with a reason, and **Release hold**: for a buyer who asks you to wait.
- **Cancel order**: only before it ships. Give the reason. The stock is released.
- **Invoice** and **Label**: a printable PDF.

The **Timeline** shows everything that happened to the order. Packing and handing over to the
courier are the warehouse's job.

## Record an advance

When a buyer pays part of the order ahead by bKash, Nagad or bank:

1. On the order, under **Advances**, press **Record advance**.
2. Choose **Paid by**, type the **Transaction id** from the buyer's message and the
   **Amount**, then press **Record**.
3. It shows **Waiting for accounts**. The amount the courier collects does not change until
   accounts verifies the payment. You cannot verify your own advance.

## Buyers and targets

- **Customers** shows a buyer's orders, deliveries, returns and lifetime value.
- **Performance** → **My performance** shows your targets for the month, week by week. If it
  says your login is not linked to an employee record, tell HR.

## If something goes wrong

- "You do not have permission to do that" means your role cannot do it. Ask your org admin.
- An order you expect is missing from **All orders**: it belongs to another agent. Ask your org
  admin.
- Ask your org admin about access, discounts above your limit and orders that seem stuck.
- Never share your password, a code, or an invite or reset link. Nobody from the company will
  ask for them.
- If you lose your phone, or see a sign-in you did not make, tell your org admin at once.
