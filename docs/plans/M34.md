# M34: Buying on the website: cart, checkout, accounts, payment, emails

Wave 3 · Depends on: M33 · Architecture §31 (new) · Decisions D-520 to D-539

> Planned on 2026-10-05. Part of the owner's instruction that the brand ERP becomes the
> website's back office: "we control order, customer … everything."

## What this is, in plain words

When a shopper checks out on the website, the order is created **in the ERP**, with the price,
discount and delivery charge the ERP works out, and the stock held at that moment. A shopper's
account (sign-up, sign-in, Google, email code, forgotten password, addresses, past orders)
lives in the ERP and is linked to the buyer record the order desk already uses. Paying online
through SSLCommerz is checked with the gateway and recorded in the books automatically. Order
emails go out from the ERP.

## Scope

1. **Cart pricing** (D-520): `POST /storefront/v1/cart/quote` takes lines (SKU id or old
   variant id, quantity), a district and thana, an optional promo code and the customer (if
   signed in) and answers each line's unit price (shelf, or the flash offer from M35 once it
   exists), what is unavailable, the discount, the delivery charge and the total, in poisha.
   The pure rule `quoteCart` lives in `packages/domain/src/storefront/` (fast-check).
   - Delivery: the website's three zones (inside Dhaka, suburbs, outside Dhaka) come from the
     ERP's delivery zone for the address; free shipping when switched on or when the items
     total reaches the threshold (the old site's rules, with 0 meaning "always free" written
     down, fixing the old site's mismatch).
2. **Checkout** (D-521): `POST /storefront/v1/checkout` (idempotency key required, signed like
   intake): re-quotes on the server and refuses with `409 CART_REPRICED` if any figure moved;
   creates the order (`channel = website`, `pending`, in the unassigned queue as today), **holds
   the stock at the web locations at once** (atomic conditional reserve; refused with
   `409 OUT_OF_STOCK` naming the lines), records promo redemption, and returns the order number
   and a one-time **order view token** for the confirmation page.
   - A held web order that an agent confirms does not reserve twice; a cancelled one releases
     its hold (state machine in `packages/domain/src/orders`, decision row).
   - The checkout form now asks for district and thana (from `GET /storefront/v1/geo`), which
     couriers need; the old free-text city and state fields go.
3. **Customer accounts** (D-522), `storefront.customer_accounts` (one per email per brand,
   linked to `sales.customers` by phone):
   - `register` (bcrypt, cost 12; a 6-digit email code, hashed, 15 minutes, 5 tries),
     `verify-email`, `resend-code` (4 per 15 minutes), `sign-in` (email and password; 8 tries
     per 10 minutes per address and per email), `google` (the website verified the Google
     token; the ERP links or creates by verified email), `forgot-password` (a 32-byte token,
     **hashed**, one hour), `reset-password`, `change-password`, `profile` (name, phone,
     addresses), `orders` (the account's orders, newest first) and `orders/:orderNo`;
   - the website keeps its own session (next-auth) and calls these server to server; the ERP
     never sets a cookie on the website;
   - guest checkouts link to a buyer by phone as the desk does; signing up with the same email
     or phone claims the past guest orders (the old site's behaviour).
4. **Order view for shoppers** (D-523): `GET /storefront/v1/orders/:orderNo?token=` (guest) or
   by account; returns only what the shopper may see, with the shopper-facing status:
   pending → "Order placed", confirmed and packed → "Processing", shipped → "Shipped",
   delivered → "Delivered", return_in_transit and returned → "Returned", partially_delivered →
   "Partly delivered", cancelled → "Cancelled".
5. **SSLCommerz** (D-524): credentials per brand, encrypted (`STOREFRONT_CREDENTIALS_KEY`), on
   **Website → Payments**. `POST /storefront/v1/payments/sslcommerz/init` creates the session
   (the worker makes the call; the route waits on it with a 15-second timeout, outside any
   transaction); success, fail, cancel and the **IPN** route validate with SSLCommerz's
   validation API and accept a payment only when the validated amount equals the order's
   payable total and the currency is BDT. A validated payment becomes an **advance** on the
   order with method `sslcommerz`, verified at once (no accounts check needed: the gateway is
   the evidence), into an SSLCommerz clearing wallet; the order's COD amount drops to what is
   left. Posting rule: Dr SSLCommerz clearing, Cr customer advances (the existing advance rule
   with a new method). A failed or cancelled payment releases the hold and cancels the order
   only if it is still pending and unpaid. Gateway fees are recorded when the settlement
   arrives (a later follow-up), not guessed.
   - The adapter is built from SSLCommerz's official developer documentation and the old
     site's working `sslcommerz-lts` flow; marked unverified until a sandbox run.
6. **Emails** (D-525): an SMTP channel in `notify` (nodemailer, the new dependency; the old
   site already used it): credentials per brand, encrypted, on **Website → Email**, with a
   test button. Templates (bn and en): email code, password reset, order placed, order
   confirmed, shipped (with tracking link), delivered, cancelled. Sent by the worker from the
   outbox, never inside a transaction; customer emails are masked in logs.
7. **Console**: the order page shows the website account and the online payment; the buyer
   profile shows the account; **Website → Payments** and **Website → Email** screens.

## Out of scope

- Flash prices and the richer discount rules (M35 plugs them into `quoteCart`).
- Moving the old site's customers and orders (M36).

## Risks

- Holding stock at checkout lets abandoned online-payment orders lock stock: an unpaid
  SSLCommerz order releases its hold and cancels after 60 minutes (a worker job).
- Money: the SSLCommerz path posts to the ledger. Ledger-auditor review; property tests that
  `cod = items − discount + delivery − advance` holds after every payment event.
- Account takeover through the Google route: the ERP only trusts it on a signed server call,
  links only verified emails, and never links a Google login to an account whose email was not
  verified.

## Acceptance checks

1. A quote and the order it becomes agree to the poisha; changing a price between the two gives
   `409 CART_REPRICED`.
2. Two shoppers racing for the last unit: one order, one `409 OUT_OF_STOCK`, stock never below
   zero (integration test on real Postgres).
3. An SSLCommerz payment whose validated amount differs from the order total is refused and
   logged; a correct one posts Dr clearing / Cr customer advances and reduces the COD amount.
4. Register, verify, sign in, reset password and Google link work end to end against the API;
   codes and tokens are stored hashed.
5. `pnpm verify` green; ledger-auditor on the money and stock diff.
