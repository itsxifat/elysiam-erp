## Scope

1. **Two digits on every label** (D-946). A label's and a slip's barcode is the order's ten digits
   and two for the lot: `00` for an order in one lot, `01` to `20` for each of several. Invoices and
   the label sheet's QR name the order, not a bag, and keep its ten digits. Everything printed
   before this still scans.
2. **Lots going to the courier on different days** (D-947). A lot left behind stays on the
   handover's list until it goes, however long after the first. A courier's own status can ship the
   order while a lot is still at the hub; that lot's scan is still taken and recorded.
3. **Lot by lot on the record** (D-948). The order page says what went in each lot, when it was
   packed, handed over and by whom, and what came back. The return station shows what went out in
   each bag. A partial delivery fills its quantities from the lots delivered.
4. **The limit's default** (D-949). The owner left the number to us: five pieces, until a brand
   chooses another in Packaging → Packing → Settings.

## Out of scope, and why: D-950

The courier and the books know one parcel per order: one consignment, one COD, one delivery
journal, one return receipt. Lot-wise tracking sits safely on top of that. These would change the
money, need the owner's rules, and were not built:

- **A consignment per lot**, each with its own number, tracking and COD.
- **Stock back per lot**: a bag received into stock before the others come back.

Until then every lot of an order carries the same consignment number and the same COD amount, now
captioned "Whole order · Collect once only", and stock comes back once, when a person presses Receive.

## Data model

None beyond M52's column, whose default became 5 (migration 0167, folded before it first ran).

## Domain

`scan-code.ts`: `soleLotScanCode`, `parseLotScanCode` and `readLotCode` read `00` as lot null.
`recording.ts`: `judgePackLabelScan` gains `old_lot_label`. `lots.ts`: `lotContents`,
`LOT_ASK_ABOVE_PIECES_DEFAULT`.

## API

- `POST /v1/packing/handover/scans` takes a lot not yet scanned on a shipped order.
- `GET /v1/packing/handover` lists every packed order with a lot scanned, however long ago.
- `GET /v1/labels/orders/:orderId`: each lot gains `items`, `packedAt`, `handedBy`, `back`.
- The return station's view: each lot gains `expected`, what went out in the bag.

## Screens

- **Order page**: the panel is headed Lots for an order in several, with each lot's record.
- **Partial delivery**: tick the lots delivered, and the quantities fill; they can still be changed.
- **Return station**: each bag says "1 of 2 pieces".

## Risks

- Labels already printed on the live brands: a one-lot order's ten digits and a lot's twelve both
  still scan. A two-lot order being packed at the deploy needs each lot's own label (D-945).
- An order of more than five pieces is now asked at Confirm from the moment this deploys (D-949).
- The older e2e suites that pack without printing a label (`handover.e2e`, parts of `orders.e2e` and
  `workflow.e2e`) were already out of date from D-928 and still are.

## The ledger audit

Fixed: a lot left behind after a courier's status shipped the order was on no list (W4, D-947);
every lot's label asked for the whole COD with nothing saying it was one collection (W2, D-950); a
failed page at the old count could be retried (W5, D-945); the partial delivery's arithmetic moved
to the domain (invariant 11). Checked on the live data, not changed: old fully scanned orders coming
back to the handover's list (W3): none on either brand at the deploy, and an unshipped order staying
in sight is the better behaviour. Wrong: a SKU on two order lines (W1): `order_lines_org_order_sku_idx`
is unique, one line per SKU per order.
