## What this is, in plain words

1. **Two new areas in the sidebar.** _Labels_ is the label desk: orders ready for their label,
   the print queue, the print log, the printers, the label terminals and the print station.
   _Packaging_ is the packing tables: orders to pack, the packing station, packed parcels, the
   handover to the courier, the packing terminals and the IP cameras. Orders no longer has Hub
   or Pack.
2. **Terminals.** Each desk is a terminal (Packing 1, Packing 2, Label 1). A supervisor assigns
   people to it, gives a packing terminal its cameras and a label terminal its printers, and
   binds the desk's computer to it once ("this computer is Packing 2"). Each terminal's card
   shows who is signed in there now, its cameras and printers, its queue, what it is packing and
   its orders per hour today.
3. **Printers.** A thermal label printer on the shop's network (TSC, Xprinter, Gprinter in TSPL;
   Zebra in ZPL; receipt printers in ESC/POS), an office printer (IPP/IPPS, sent a PDF), or the
   browser of the label desk's computer (the print station). Each has its paper size and what it
   prints. Test print sends a test page.
4. **Automatic printing.** The moment the courier gives an order its consignment number, its
   label (each lot's) prints on the label terminal for its location, the online one with the
   shortest queue, with no click. On by default once a printer exists; one switch per brand.
5. **The log.** Every print and reprint is kept: what, which lot, which printer and terminal,
   who or automatic, when, how many copies, and whether it printed. A reprint needs a reason.
   Each order shows "printed 3 times, last 17 Oct 14:02 by Rahim at Label 1". The log is
   searchable and exports to CSV.
6. **Lots.** The label printer or the packer sets how many bags an order goes in. Each bag gets
   its own slip and label marked "LOT 2 OF 3" with its own barcode. At the packing station the
   pieces go into lot 1, then lot 2…; the order is packed only when every lot is closed. At the
   handover each lot's label is scanned; the order goes to the courier only when all are.

## Build

- **Domain** (`@elysium/domain/packing`, pure, property-tested): `lots.ts` (lot codes, the lot
  count rule, closing a lot, finishing, the handover) and `printing.ts` (job states, choosing
  the terminal, the auto-print default, a printer's state, the print summary, orders per hour).
- **Contracts** (`labels.ts`): terminals, members, cameras, binding, printers (with the
  private-LAN rule), print requests, the queue and log query, the auto-print settings, the
  station's result, lots and the handover; error codes; the `PackingTerminal` subject and its
  grants; the `labels.job_changed` event.
- **Database** (`packing` schema; migrations `0153_m46_labels_packaging_schema`, drizzle-kit, and
  `0154_m46_labels_packaging_policies`, by hand): `terminals`, `terminal_members`,
  `terminal_devices`, `printers`, `print_jobs`, `label_settings`, `label_orders`, `order_lots`,
  `lot_closures`, `handover_scans`, `session_cameras`; `cameras.terminal_id`,
  `sessions.terminal_id`, `scans.lot_no`. RLS ENABLE and FORCE on each, grants per table, triggers
  that keep identities and the print log fixed, M38c's camera-part rule widened to a terminal's
  cameras, and the backfill of today's booked orders into the ready list.
- **Server** (packing module): `TerminalsService` (+ controller `/v1/terminals`),
  `PrintersService` and `PrintingService` (+ `/v1/labels`), `LotsService` (+ `/v1/packing/orders/
:orderId/lots`, `/v1/packing/sessions/:id/lots/close`, `/v1/packing/handover`); the packing
  station takes the bound terminal, its cameras and lots; the camera process cuts every camera of
  a session; the printer drivers (`printers/`: PNG reader, raster, TSPL, ZPL, ESC/POS, IPP, the
  socket and HTTP transport, the destination rule, the render). Worker jobs `labels.auto_print`,
  `labels.send`, `labels.printer_status`.
- **Orders and courier**: `ParcelObserverRegistry` (the courier's booking transaction tells
  packing), `HandoverGuardRegistry` (a multi-lot order's Ship needs every lot scanned),
  `OrdersApi.documentForPrint` and `markSlipsPrinted`, `PackingFacts.locationId` and
  `slipPrintedAt`, the lot on M43's slip and label.
- **Console**: `routes/labels/` (both pages, the panels, the print station, the binding and the
  order page's print record); the packing station embedded in Packaging with lots and the
  terminal's cameras; Orders' Hub and Pack links removed (old links redirect); the till's strings
  moved to their own i18n section to keep the first download in budget.
