# M10: Order core

Phase 1 · Effort MAX · Depends on: M07, M08 · Architecture §7, §9

> Written on 2026-09-30, after the build. M10 was built on 2026-09-26 without a
> plan file. This records the plan as executed.

## Scope

Orders move through one proven state machine, and every step keeps stock and the
books in step. This milestone owns customers, delivery zones, orders and lines,
the transitions, the Bangla invoice and packing slip, and the manual delivery,
partial delivery, return and exchange postings that stand in until couriers.

## Out of scope

- The order desk screens, buyer list and public intake (M11).
- Courier booking, status sync and settlement (M12, M15).
- The fraud score. M10 defines the `FRAUD_GATE` port and M13 binds it.
- Promo codes and buyer profiles (M16).

## Data model

Postgres schema `sales` (Drizzle folder `orders`), migrations 0039 to 0044.

- `customers`, `customer_addresses`: phone stored normalised.
- `couriers`: the courier party row. M12 builds around it (D-171).
- `delivery_zones`, `delivery_zone_areas`: D-009 placeholder charges, seeded.
- `order_counters`: per-org order numbering.
- `orders`, `order_lines`, `order_events`.
- 0041 holds the integrity triggers. Lines may be deleted only while an order is
  pending, confirmed or on hold (D-102).
- 0044 adds line editing.

## Domain functions and what tests them

In `packages/domain/src/orders`:

- `state.ts`: the transition table. Every (from, to) pair is asserted allowed
  or forbidden (invariant 7).
- `totals.ts`: COD = items − discount + delivery − advance. Property tested, and
  a negative result is refused. Also `computePartialDelivery` and
  `applicableAdvance`.
- `phone.ts`: a Bangladesh mobile rule, including Bangla digits (D-170).
- `numbering.ts`.

Posting rules: `order-delivered.ts`, `order-return.ts`, `order-exchange.ts`,
`customer-advance.ts`. The golden-books scenario is in `order-posting.test.ts`.

## API, events and screens

- Routes: `/v1/orders` and its transitions, `/v1/customers`,
  `/v1/delivery-zones`.
- `orders.api.ts` is the interface for courier, fraud and POS.
- Events: `order.created`, `order.assigned`, `order.claimed`,
  `order.status_changed`.
- PDFs: invoice and packing slip through Gotenberg, in Bangla.
- Screens belong to M11.

## Decisions

D-098 to D-102, D-112 to D-114, D-170 to D-172. The ones that change behaviour:
one advance per order (D-101), an order event may be dated only today or
yesterday in Dhaka (D-112), an order ships only from a warehouse or showroom
location (D-113).

## Risks that mattered

Three money defects were found in review and fixed with regression tests.

- **An exchange that gave goods away free.** The replacement dispatched with no
  price difference posted.
- **A partial delivery that booked the buyer's prepayment as income.** It now
  applies only the advance attributable to the delivered lines.
- **A zero-revenue delivery that could never post.** The revenue line is now
  conditional on a positive net.

Also: a return must be valued at the dispatch cost, not the current WAC. The
first audit found no test could tell the two apart. `orders.db.test.ts` now
moves the average cost between dispatch and return and asserts the difference.
