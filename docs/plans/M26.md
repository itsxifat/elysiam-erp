# M26: Company loans

Phase 5 · Effort XHIGH · Depends on: M08, M16 · Architecture §7, §15, §20

> Planned on 2026-10-02, before the build, under continuous build mode (no approval
> pause; the plan is still written first).

## Scope

Money a brand borrows, and paying it back:

1. **Lenders and loans.** A lender (a bank, a person, or other) is a per-brand row, and its
   id is the ledger party on `loan_payable` (§7: party type `lender`). A loan records the
   lender, principal, annual rate, method (`flat` or `reducing`, D-020), tenure in months,
   the wallet the principal arrived in, and the first due date. Recording it posts
   **Dr W / Cr Loan payable (lender)** and writes its schedule, in one transaction.
2. **Schedules.** A pure function in `packages/domain/src/loans` produces the instalments:
   flat (interest on the original principal, spread evenly) or reducing balance (equal
   instalments, interest on what is still owed). Integer poisha throughout, through
   `packages/domain/src/money`; Σ principal = the loan amount exactly.
3. **Repayments.** One instalment at a time, oldest first, posting **Dr Loan payable
   (principal) + Dr Interest expense / Cr W** through `ledger.post()`. A repayment is money
   leaving the company, so it goes through approvals whenever the brand's threshold says
   so — a new `loan_repayment` type, governed like a supplier payment.
4. **Reminders.** A daily job (10:00 Dhaka, §20) reminds the brand N days before each due
   date: in-app (a realtime event into the console's notification inbox) and by SMS through
   the notify module, **exactly once per instalment**.
5. **Statements.** Per loan: the schedule with each instalment's state, every repayment
   split into principal and interest with its journal, the totals, and the ledger's own
   loan payable balance for the lender beside the schedule's figure.

Console: a Loans screen (lenders, loans, a new-loan form with the schedule previewed by the
server) and a loan statement with a pay action per instalment.

## Out of scope

- Partial payments, prepayment, early settlement, penalties, rescheduling, and a changed
  rate mid-loan. An instalment is paid whole, as scheduled (D-297). Follow-ups.
- Loans taken before go-live. The receipt posts at the server's clock, so an old loan
  cannot be entered without double-counting its cash; M14a has no loan importer. Follow-up,
  owner's decision (D-296).
- Reversing a posted repayment. A loan nothing has been paid on can be voided (D-304,
  added after the ledger audit); a posted repayment cannot be undone yet. Follow-up.
- Loans the company _gives_ (staff loans are M19's; there is no other kind).
- Daily-accrual interest, non-monthly instalments, and day-count conventions (D-294).

## Data model

New schema `loans`, all tenant tables with `org_id`, RLS ENABLE and FORCE, composite
tenant foreign keys (D-047), minimal grants. Migrations 0089 (generated) and 0090
(`--custom`: policies, grants, triggers); after the ledger audit, 0091 (generated: the void
columns and unique journal indexes) and 0092 (`--custom`: the void trigger and the
approval-bound payment triggers). They will collide with M20's numbers and the integrator
renumbers.

- `loans.lenders`: name, kind (`bank` | `person` | `other`), notes. UPDATE to rename;
  never DELETE (journals name the lender as their party).
- `loans.loans`: lender, principal, `rate_bps` (annual, 1200 = 12%), method, tenure,
  `received_on`, `first_due_on`, `reminder_days` (0–30, default 3), optional
  `reminder_phone` (the brand's own staff number for the SMS), wallet account, receipt
  journal, memo. **Append-only**: a loan's terms are what was signed.
- `loans.instalments`: seq, due date, principal, interest. **Append-only.** A deferred
  constraint trigger checks at commit that a loan has exactly `tenure_months` instalments
  and that their principal sums to the loan's.
- `loans.payments`: instalment, wallet, principal and interest (equal to the instalment's,
  checked by a trigger), status `pending_approval` → `posted` | `rejected`, approval,
  journal. One live payment per instalment (partial unique index). UPDATE only for that
  one transition (trigger); never DELETE.
- `loans.reminders`: one row per instalment (unique), the date it was sent, and what
  happened to the SMS (`queued`, `suppressed`, `no_phone`, `failed`) with the message id.
  **Append-only.** The unique row is the "once" of check 3, in the database.
- `approvals.approval_type` gains `loan_repayment`.

## Domain functions and what tests them

In `packages/domain/src/loans/schedule.ts`:

- `loanSchedule({ principalPoisha, rateBps, method, tenureMonths, firstDueOn })`.
  - **Flat**: total interest = principal × rate × months / 12, rounded once (half away
    from zero); principal and interest each spread with `allocate` (largest remainder).
  - **Reducing**: the instalment `P·r·(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1)` with `r = rate/12`,
    computed as an exact bigint ratio and rounded once; each month's interest is the
    outstanding balance × r, rounded; principal is the instalment less interest; **the last
    instalment takes whatever principal remains** (last-instalment rounding). A zero rate
    splits the principal with `allocate`.
  - Due dates: `firstDueOn` plus k months, from the first due date's day (31 Jan → 28/29
    Feb → 31 Mar), so the dates never drift.
- Tests (`loans.test.ts`): check 1 is a fast-check property over random principal, rate,
  tenure and method — Σ principal = the loan amount, every part ≥ 0, Σ instalment = Σ
  principal + Σ interest; check 2 is a reducing-balance table computed by hand
  (৳10,000 at 12% over 3 months, worked line by line in the test) and the published
  ৳1,00,000 / 12% / 12-month table (instalment ৳8,884.88). Flat worked example too.

Posting rules in `packages/domain/src/posting/company-loan.ts`, each with a fast-check
balance property and a worked example:

- `companyLoanReceivedDraft`: Dr W / Cr Loan payable (lender). Key
  `company_loan:<loanId>:received`.
- `loanInstalmentPaidDraft`: Dr Loan payable (lender, principal) + Dr Interest expense /
  Cr W. Key `loan_instalment:<instalmentId>:paid` — keyed by the instalment, so a second
  payment for one instalment can never post a second journal, whatever the rows say.

## API, jobs, events and screens

Subject `CompanyLoan` (org_admin and accounts at org scope, already in the §6 matrix).

- `GET /v1/loans/lenders`, `POST /v1/loans/lenders` (read / create).
- `POST /v1/loans/preview`: the schedule for proposed terms, nothing written (read).
- `GET /v1/loans`: each loan with outstanding principal and the next instalment (read).
- `POST /v1/loans`: record a loan — receipt journal and schedule (create).
- `GET /v1/loans/:id`: the statement (read).
- `POST /v1/loans/:id/payments`: pay the next instalment (update). Below the threshold it
  posts at once; otherwise it waits for a checker, whose approval posts it in the
  deciding transaction and whose rejection marks it rejected (a rejection hook added to
  the approvals module, D-299).
- `POST /v1/loans/:id/void`: void a loan nothing has been paid on, reversing its receipt
  (update; added after the ledger audit, D-304).
- Jobs: `loans.schedule_reminders` (global, cron `0 10 * * *` Dhaka) fans out one
  `loans.remind_instalments` per brand (exclusive, singleton per brand per day).
- Event: `loan.instalment_due` (loan id, instalment id, seq, due date, amount) to
  `org:role:accounts` and `org:role:org_admin`; the console inbox shows it.
- Notify: template key `loan.instalment_due` (transactional), queued through `NotifyApi`
  in the reminder's own transaction — the SMS itself is sent by `notify.send` after commit.
- Console: `/loans` and `/loans/$loanId`; a nav entry beside Investors; bn + en.

## Risks

- **A poisha lost to rounding.** Σ principal must equal the loan to the poisha, or the
  loan never reaches zero in the ledger. The last instalment absorbs it; a property test
  and a deferred database trigger both check it.
- **A repayment posted twice.** A retried request or two clicks. The payment id is
  client-generated and replayed; one live payment per instalment is a unique index; and
  the journal key is the instalment's.
- **An approval that pays a different instalment.** The executor refuses unless the
  payment names this approval and the approval's amount equals the payment's.
- **A reminder sent twice, or never.** Two racing runs: the unique reminder row makes the
  loser's transaction (its message included) roll back. A run that missed days: anything
  inside the window and not yet reminded is reminded once on the next run.
- **An SMS inside a transaction.** Never: the reminder writes a notify message row and its
  job in the transaction; `notify.send` calls the gateway after commit (invariant 6).

## Status on 2026-10-02

Built, audited and verified on `m26-company-loans` (not merged).

What exists:

- **Lenders and loans** (`modules/loans/loans.*`): per-brand lenders; a loan posts its receipt
  (Dr W / Cr Loan payable, lender) and writes its schedule in one transaction. A replay with
  the same id must match every term, or it is a conflict.
- **Schedules** (`packages/domain/src/loans`): flat and reducing balance, integer poisha,
  Σ principal exact, also held by a deferred trigger.
- **Repayments** (`payments.service.ts`): the oldest unpaid instalment, whole; posted at once
  under the brand's threshold, otherwise `pending_approval` until a checker approves (posts in
  the deciding transaction) or rejects (released through the new rejection hook, D-299).
- **Reminders** (`reminders.service.ts`): `loans.schedule_reminders` fans out per brand; one
  reminder row per instalment, in-app and SMS through notify.
- **Statements**: `GET /v1/loans/:id` and the console screen, with the ledger's figure for
  the lender beside the schedules'.
- **Void** (D-304, added after the ledger audit): `POST /v1/loans/:id/void` reverses the
  receipt of a loan nothing has been paid on; a void loan is never reminded or repaid.

Deviations from this plan: the void route, migrations 0091 and 0092, and the database's own
binding of a repayment to its approval and journal were added after the ledger audit.

Audits: ledger-auditor findings (W1 an approval that could post nothing, W2 payment state
resting on application code alone, W3 no correction path for a mistaken loan, N4 one row per
journal, plus race and replay tests) fixed with tests; acceptance-auditor NOT READY with 2
gaps (the architecture module row; no real-stack run of the touched inbox), both closed.
Re-audit of the fixes: not yet run.
