## Scope

1. **Edit an order** before packing (pending, confirmed, on hold): `/orders/$orderId/edit`. Add,
   remove and change items; delivery charge; the person's own discount; the offers panel.
   Saved with `PATCH /v1/orders/:id` and the new `discountIds` (D-921).
2. **Change a packed order**: the same screen; its save cancels the packed order and creates a
   confirmed replacement in one transaction, `POST /v1/orders/:id/replace` (D-922). Each order
   names the other on its page.
3. **Return items** from a parcel with the courier: `/orders/$orderId/return`. Mark pieces
   returned (or scan them), re-price what was kept with the offers panel and a new delivery
   charge, and record a partial delivery (or the whole parcel returning) through the existing
   routes (D-924).
4. **Offers panel** for both: every running offer for the buyer, which qualify for the items as
   they stand and why the others do not, `POST /v1/orders/:id/offers` (D-921).
5. **Product picker** with photos, every size and colour with price, SKU and stock, every
   product a page at a time, search by name, SKU, parent SKU, barcode or slug,
   `GET /v1/orders/picker` (D-923). Also on the order desk ("Browse products").
6. **Scan to search everywhere** (D-925): a scan fills the page's search box wherever focus
   is; the editor screens take every scan, and a scan typed into one of their quantity or
   charge boxes is taken back out.

## Out of scope (follow-ups)

- Replacing a packed order that holds an advance (refused for now; the cancel would make the
  advance a refund owed).
- A changed delivery charge on a return is booked through Sales discounts, not delivery income.
- A paid return (the buyer pays only the delivery charge on a whole-parcel return).
- Telling the courier the new cash-on-delivery amount through its API (no verified endpoint).
- Re-pricing flash-sale lines on an edit (still refused, as before).
