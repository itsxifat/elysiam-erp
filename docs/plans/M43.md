## What this is, in plain words

1. **New order documents.** The invoice (A4), the packing slip (A4, or A5 with `?paper=a5`) and
   the parcel label (4 × 6 in) are redrawn: a large order number with the barcode the packing
   station scans, the cash to collect in a heavy box, the recipient's name, phone and full
   address large and bold, the courier with its consignment and tracking numbers, a clean items
   table (item, SKU, size, colour, quantity, and on the invoice the unit price and line total),
   the totals (subtotal, discount, delivery, total, paid, to collect), the order date, channel
   and notes, and the brand's name with its logo when the brand has one. Inter, tabular figures,
   1 px rules, black ink so a laser or thermal printer prints it cleanly.
2. **English everywhere.** Every document, SMS, email, till receipt and website account message
   is formal English; district and thana names come from `name_en`; money on paper and in
   messages reads `Tk 1,250.00`. The Bangla catalogues and every language switch (console, till,
   portal, owner console) are gone.

## Scope

- `apps/server/src/modules/orders/documents.service.ts`: the three documents, rebuilt; size and
  colour per line from a new `CatalogApi.variantValues`; the courier from `parcelTracking`; the
  logo from the brand block or the website's logo (a registry the storefront fills, as the
  courier booking port does), its bytes from a new `FilesApi.publicImage`, uploaded with the
  document. A5 for the slip.
- `files/pdf/fonts.ts`: Inter only (the vendored latin and latin-ext variable files); the sample
  invoice (`invoice.template.ts`) in English.
- Same English, Inter and `Tk` style: courier labels, the QR label sheet, stickers' price,
  payslips, till receipts.
- Customer messages: SMS from `body_en` only (the template editor shows one English body;
  `body_bn` is left in the table, filled with the same text, D-755), emails English only,
  storefront account and order emails, `statusLabel`, the CRM and loan reminder call sites.
- `packages/i18n`: `LOCALES = ['en']`, no `CUSTOMER_LOCALE`, `bn.ts` and `sections/*.bn.ts`
  deleted, Bangla digit and month tables removed. Console, till, portal and owner console:
  switches removed, every `locale === 'bn'` branch gone. `scripts/check-i18n.mjs`: English only.
- Tests: every document rendered with no character in U+0980 to U+09FF; exact PDF page sizes
  against Gotenberg; the slip's barcode decoded back to the order number and opened at the real
  packing station; SMS, email and receipt renders with no Bangla.

- Docs: CLAUDE.md (i18n line), architecture §22 and §24, decisions D-750 to D-759, the training
