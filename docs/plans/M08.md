# M08: Accounts and wallets

Phase 1 · Effort MAX · Depends on: M03, M05 · Architecture §6, §7

## Scope

The client's daily cash picture: wallets per org and branch, cash in and out by
category, transfers, petty cash, payables and receivables with aging, the five
reports, super-admin consolidation, and daily closing.

This is the milestone the client's own words point at most directly — "keep
track of all money". M03 built the ledger; this is the surface a human uses it
through, and every number on it has to reconcile to a journal.

## The check that matters

Check 1: **a scripted day of entries leaves every cash book closing balance
equal to its ledger balance, and the trial balance at zero.** Everything else in
this milestone is UI over that property. If a cash book can disagree with the
ledger, the reports are decoration.

The way that goes wrong is a report computed from its own table rather than from
journal lines — a `wallets.balance_poisha` column kept in step by application
code. It will drift, and the drift is silent until a count. Balances are derived
from `ledger.journal_lines`, and if a cached balance is ever needed for
performance it is a materialized view with a test that compares it to the
derivation.

## Traps

- **Petty cash without a receipt.** Check 2: rejected, and the receipt is a
  _private_ file needing permission to view. M04's files slice provides the
  private bucket and the authorize-then-sign rule; this milestone must not
  shortcut to a public URL because it is easier.
- **Consolidation through the wrong role.** Check 3 and §5 exception 3: the
  super-admin consolidated view reads through `erp_analytics`, read-only, and
  every write stays scoped to exactly one org. A consolidated view built with
  `withOrg` in a loop would work and would be the wrong shape the day someone
  adds a write to it.
- **Closing variance posted without approval.** Check 4. `closing_variance` is
  already an approval type, and §6 gives it to org_admin and to a branch checker
  under threshold — and _not_ to accounts, which M03's audit corrected. The
  posting is Dr or Cr cash over/short, per §7.
- **A wallet transfer that loses the bank charge.** §7: Dr W2 + Dr bank charges /
  Cr W1. The charge is a third line, and a two-line transfer silently absorbs it.

## Out of scope

Courier settlements (M12), payroll payment (M19), investor and loan movements
(M25, M26). Each posts through `ledger.post()` with its own rule when it lands.

## Assumptions to record

- Aging bucket boundaries (30/60/90?), which §7 does not give.
- Whether a branch manager may see another branch's wallet in the consolidated
  branch P&L, given §6 grants them `branch` scope on `Wallet` and `LedgerReport`.

## Follow-up build: the missing money screens (planned 2026-09-30)

Every route below exists, is in the route matrix and is tested. What is missing is
the surface a person uses them through. No server change is planned; if one turns
out to be needed it gets its own decision row.

**Structure.** `/accounts` becomes a section with its own sub-navigation, like
Stock and Supply:

| Route                    | Screen                                                                                                                | Reads and writes                                              | Who sees it                    |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| `/accounts`              | Wallets, cash in and out, daily closings (exists)                                                                     | `/v1/accounts/wallets`, `cash-entries`, `closings`            | `read Wallet`                  |
| `/accounts/transfers`    | Move money between two wallets, with an optional bank charge as a third line                                          | `POST /v1/accounts/transfers`                                 | `update Wallet`                |
| `/accounts/wallets/$id`  | One wallet's cash book: opening, every line with what it was against, running and closing balance, for a chosen range | `/v1/ledger/wallets/:id/cash-book`                            | `read Wallet`                  |
| `/accounts/reports`      | Profit and loss for a chosen range and branch; balance sheet as of a date; payables and receivables aging             | `/v1/accounts/reports/*`                                      | `read LedgerReport`            |
| `/accounts/ledger`       | Trial balance as of a date; day book for a range; one account's ledger                                                | `/v1/ledger/trial-balance`, `day-book`, `accounts/:id/ledger` | `read LedgerReport`            |
| `/accounts/periods`      | Months, open or closed; close a month; request a reopen with a reason (maker-checker)                                 | `/v1/ledger/periods`, `close`, `reopen`                       | `read` / `update LedgerPeriod` |
| `/accounts/consolidated` | Every brand's wallets and P&L side by side                                                                            | `/v1/accounts/reports/consolidated-*`                         | super admin only               |

**Rules the screens must keep.**

- Every figure is the server's. Nothing is summed in the browser.
- Cost is present or absent, never zero. Every report says when `costWithheld` is
  true, and the balance sheet shows its `differencePoisha` rather than forcing the
  two sides equal.
- A transfer types taka and converts with `takaToPoisha`. Its id is generated
  once per attempt, so a retried request is the same transfer.
- Ranges and dates live in the URL, so a link to "P&L for August at Gulshan"
  opens on it.
- Loading, empty, error and permission-denied states on every screen, and every
  string in bn and en.

**Tests.** A unit test per pure helper (range presets, the transfer form's
validation). A real-stack browser spec: an accounts user transfers between two
wallets and sees both cash books move, opens the P&L and the trial balance, and
closes a month. Axe on each new screen.
