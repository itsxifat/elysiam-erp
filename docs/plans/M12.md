## Scope

Cash held by a courier is tracked from booking, through delivery, to the
settlement that lands in the right wallet. Nothing the courier says can move
money twice.

## Out of scope

- Pathao and RedX adapters, zone rules, bulk booking, tracking page, the stuck
  queue and automatic settlement fetch (M15).
- SMS to the buyer on status change (M16).

## Data model

Schema `courier`, migrations 0048 to 0052.

- `courier_accounts`: per org, credentials encrypted with
  `COURIER_CREDENTIALS_KEY`.
- `consignments`, `consignment_events`: the parcel and its status history.
- `webhook_deliveries`: the dedupe record for webhooks and polls.
- `return_receipts` and lines: inspection as sellable or damaged.
- `settlements`, `settlement_lines`.
- 0051 and 0052 bind a settlement to its approval and to the consignments it
  pays.

## Domain functions and what tests them

In `packages/domain/src/courier`:

- `status.ts`: each provider's status vocabulary mapped to a normalised status
  and to an order trigger. The vocabularies are typed out with their source.
- `booking.ts`: parcel input and whole-taka conversion.
- `settlement.ts`: matching, exceptions, the D-037 closed-period rule, and the
  settlement journal.
- Posting rule `courier-settlement.ts`.

## API, events, jobs and screens

- Routes under `/v1/courier`: accounts, parcels, handover, receive-return,
  settlements with approval, the COD reconciliation report.
- `POST /v1/courier/webhooks/:orgId/:provider` is public and signed.
- Event: `delivery.booking_failed`.
- Jobs: booking with retries and a dead-letter queue, and the status poll.
- Adapter interface `CourierAdapter`, a fake adapter, and the Steadfast adapter.
- Console: `/courier`, `/courier/settlements`, `/courier/returns`,
  `/courier/parcels/$consignmentId`. These were built with M15.

## Decisions

D-122 to D-127, D-129, D-130, D-171, D-176. The ones that protect cash: a
collection is recorded only once the order's transition has posted (D-129), a
settlement whose charges exceed its collections is refused (D-125), and an
unverified adapter cannot be activated without an explicit switch (D-176).

## Risks that mattered

Four critical findings from the ledger audit, all fixed with tests.

- **Every courier webhook booked the full COD**, whatever the courier said it
  collected.
- **The polling cron could never see an org** through its own RLS, so polling
  silently did nothing.
- **A settlement under the approval threshold could never post.**
- **The status mapping test was circular.** It took the vocabulary from the map
  it was testing. It now reads a typed list with its source.
