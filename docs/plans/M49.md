## What this is, in plain words

1. **A messaging-style inbox.** Three rounded cards on the canvas: the list (title, live dot, your
   status, All / Mine / Unassigned, a pill search, filter pills, rounded rows with unread dots),
   the thread (a one-line header with round actions, the chat on the canvas, rounder bubbles, one
   rounded reply field with the voice button and Send) and the customer panel.
2. **An organised customer panel.** A header with the person, their stage, number and Call,
   Create order and Customer page; then three tabs: **Customer** (overview, details, numbers,
   addresses, recent orders, chat tags), **Order** (the side card, the draft order and its picker,
   products of interest) and **Notes** (the pinned note and the chat's notes). Each section is a
   card.
3. **The real customer in the chat.** The buyer a chat is linked to is the customer management
   record: their details with the email, every number with the staff's notes (add, make primary,
   edit, retire), the address book (add, edit, make default, remove), edited from the inbox by
   whoever answers the chat. Before the link: the people already on the number ("This is them"),
   Save as customer, and Not this customer to undo a wrong link.
4. **Every product.** The picker and "Interested in" list every match, loading more as the
   results scroll in their own area.
5. **Back the other way.** The buyer page gets Details (with the email), Addresses, and a Chats
   card with the buyer's conversations and the notes written in them.
