# M15: Multi-courier, webhooks, tracking (Track A)

Phase 2 · Effort XHIGH · Depends on: M12 · Gate: G5 · Architecture §9, §21

> Written on 2026-09-30, after the build. M15 was built on 2026-09-29 without a
> plan file. This records the plan as executed.

## Scope

More than one courier per brand, without losing track of whose cash is whose.
This adds the Pathao and RedX adapters, zone rules that recommend a courier,
bulk booking and labels, a public tracking page, automatic settlement fetch, a
performance report, and the stuck-parcel queue.

## Out of scope

- Routing a booking by zone rule. The rules recommend only (D-152). The orders
  module consulting them when an order is taken is a follow-up.
- SMS tracking links to the buyer (M16).
- A per-org staleness threshold for the stuck queue.

## Data model

Schema `courier`, migrations 0065 to 0067.

- `selection_rules`: zone to courier, ordered.
- `consignment_escalations`: the note trail for a stuck parcel.
- `settlement_fetch_runs`: one row per fetch, keyed by a fingerprint of the
  canonical statement plus the courier's reference.
- 0067 adds a trigger: a collected amount is recorded only on a delivery
  (D-153).

## Domain functions and what tests them

In `packages/domain/src/courier`, tested in `m15.test.ts`:

- `selection.ts`: `selectCourier` by zone rule.
- `stuck.ts`: the staleness rule, in Asia/Dhaka calendar days.
- `tracking.ts`: what the public page may show, and the uniform miss.
- `performance.ts`: delivery rate, average days and return rate by zone.
- `settlement-fetch.ts`: the statement fingerprint.
- `status.ts` gains a complete mapping table for each new adapter.

## API, jobs and screens

- Staff routes: selection rules and preview, bulk booking, labels, stuck queue
  with re-poll, escalation and export, performance report, settlement fetch and
  its runs.
- Public: `POST /v1/track`. Brand, order number and the last four phone digits.
  It keeps the CSRF check because it is a browser route.
- Jobs: settlement fetch daily at 06:30 Dhaka (D-158).
- Console: `/courier`, `/courier/stuck`, `/courier/performance`,
  `/courier/settlements`, `/courier/returns`, the parcel timeline.
- Portal: the tracking page in `apps/portal/src/tracking`.

## Decisions

D-135 and D-152 to D-160. The ones that protect cash: an order cannot move to
another courier while a parcel is booked (D-135), an unknown courier status
never replaces the tracking status and lists the parcel as stuck at once
(D-154), Pathao COD must be whole taka and is refused rather than rounded
(D-157), and a fetched settlement needs approval like an upload (D-158).

## Risks that mattered

- **Receivable on the wrong party.** Delivery debits the order's courier and
  settlement credits the consignment's. After a courier change the org total
  still agrees and only two unpairable variances show it. D-135 forbids the
  change.
- **A paid return recorded as a collection.** The settlement line matched and
  credited receivable with no debit behind it. The 0067 trigger refuses it.
- **An unverified adapter left switched on.** The switch is now read at every
  use (D-155).
- **The tracking page as an oracle.** Every miss returns the same 200 body and
  spends the rate limit the same way.
- **The tracking rate limiter resolved before the Valkey limiter existed.** The
  limiter is now built before the application.
