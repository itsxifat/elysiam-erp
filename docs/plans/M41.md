> The owner's brief of 2026-10-11: the sales desk (orders, customers, messages) and the
> fulfilment hub (slips, packing, labels, courier handover) work about 2 km apart. One person
> handles each order at a time; the first to see an order takes it, and anyone else who touches
> it is warned at every step. Auto-assign offers new orders evenly to the people actively using
> the ERP, and even an offered order has to be accepted, like a ride offer.

## What this is, in plain words

1. **Ownership.** An order's owner is `sales.orders.assigned_to`. Opening an unassigned order
   shows "Take this order"; taking it is a deliberate click (GETs stay free of side effects).
   Once someone has it, every desk action by anyone else (edit, confirm, hold, release hold,
   cancel, exchange, record an advance, log a call, bulk confirm) is refused with
   `ORDER_HANDLED_BY_OTHER` naming the owner, unless the request carries
   `x-acknowledge-owner: 1`. Then it proceeds, is audited as `order.acted_over_owner`, and the
   owner is told in real time. The hub's fulfilment moves (pack, ship, deliver, returns, the
   packing station, the courier) are the hub's work and are not gated by the desk owner.
2. **Take, release, hand over, reassign.** Two people taking one order at once: exactly one wins,
   and the other is told who has it. The owner can release the order back to New or hand it to a
   colleague; org_admin, branch_manager and super admins can reassign anyone's order in scope.
3. **Verification calls.** Each call attempt is logged (reached, no answer, busy, wrong number,
   call back at a time) with a note. The queue shows the attempt count and the call-back time,
   and due call-backs come first.
4. **Presence.** The console reports, about every 30 s, whether its tab is visible and how long
   since the last input. Active: visible and input within 2 minutes. Idle: otherwise. Away: no
   report for more than 90 s. "Not taking orders" is a switch the person sets. Only the ERP's
   own tab is observed.
5. **Desk profiles.** Each staff member's work types (orders, messages) and, for hub staff,
   the hub's stock location, edited on the Staff page.
6. **Auto-assign** (Settings → Order workflow; off by default, ASSUMED). A new order is offered
   to the active, not-paused person with the orders work type and the fewest open orders, under
   the brand's cap. They accept or decline within a countdown (45 s by default). A decline or an
   expiry moves it to the next person; with nobody eligible it waits in New. Accepting is one
   conditional statement: the offer is live and the order is still unassigned.
7. **Queues.** Sales desk: New, Mine, Verifying, Call-backs due, Confirmed today (with the hub
   stage of each). Hub, at the person's hub location: To print, Packing, Packed, Handed to
   courier; bulk slip printing (the existing documents endpoint, then "mark printed"); a
   scan-to-hand-over list that ships through the existing transition.
8. **Team board** for supervisors: each person's presence, open orders, confirmed today and
   outstanding offers, with reassignment.

Out of scope: conversations (M42 reuses the engine and presence), the packing station's own
take-over warning (a follow-up), SMS or push notifications of offers, per-branch team boards.

## Design

### Domain (pure, tested; invariant 11)

- `packages/domain/src/assignment/` (`@elysium/domain/assignment`):
  - `presenceState({ lastSeenAt, lastActiveAt, paused, now })` → `active | idle | away`, and
    `available` (active and not paused). Thresholds `PRESENCE_AWAY_AFTER_MS` (90 s) and
    `PRESENCE_IDLE_AFTER_MS` (120 s).
  - `pickAssignee({ kind, candidates, declinedBy, now })`, generic for `'order' | 'conversation'`:
    eligible = available, has the kind's work type, open load < max load, not in `declinedBy`,
    no live offer; least open load, then longest since last offered, then user id.
  - Property tests (fast-check): never idle, away, paused, over-cap, already declined or busy;
    always the least loaded eligible; repeated offer-and-accept rounds keep loads within one.
- `packages/domain/src/orders/ownership.ts`:
  - `ownershipVerdict({ ownerId, callerId, acknowledged })` → `free | refused | acknowledged`.
  - `assignmentRule({ ownerId, callerId, target, supervisor })` → allowed act or refusal.
  - `CALL_OUTCOMES`, `callAttemptProblem(...)`, `hubStageOf(...)`.

### Data (migrations generated by `pnpm db:generate`, renumbered at integration)

- New schema `team` (module `team`):
  - `team.presence` (org_id, user_id PK pair, last_seen_at, last_active_at, paused, paused_at,
    updated_at): one row per person per brand, upserted by the heartbeat.
  - `team.desk_profiles` (org_id, user_id PK pair, work_types text[], hub_location_id with the
    (org_id, id) FK to `org.locations`, updated_by, timestamps).
- `sales` additions (module `orders`):
  - `sales.orders`: `assigned_at`, `call_attempts` (int, default 0), `last_call_outcome`,
    `last_call_at`, `call_back_at`, `slip_printed_at`.
  - `sales.order_offers` (id, org_id, order_id → (org_id, id) FK, user_id, status
    offered|accepted|declined|expired|withdrawn, offered_at, expires_at, responded_at); partial
    unique indexes: one live offer per order, one per person.
  - `sales.call_attempts` (id, org_id, order_id FK, outcome, call_back_at, note, called_by,
    called_at): INSERT and SELECT only.
  - `sales.workflow_settings` (org_id PK, auto_assign, offer_seconds, max_open, updated_by).
- RLS ENABLE + FORCE with the standard policy on all five tables; minimum grants.

### API (every route: an ability, a route-matrix entry and a fixture)

| Route                                           | Ability                       | What                               |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| `POST /v1/team/presence`                        | `update Presence`             | heartbeat `{visible, idleSeconds}` |
| `POST /v1/team/presence/pause`                  | `update Presence`             | `{paused}` ("Not taking orders")   |
| `GET /v1/team/me`                               | `read Presence`               | my presence and desk profile       |
| `GET /v1/team/profiles`                         | `read User`                   | the Staff page's desk profiles     |
| `PUT /v1/team/profiles/:userId`                 | `update User`                 | work types, hub location           |
| `POST /v1/orders/:id/release`                   | `update Order`                | owner or supervisor                |
| `POST /v1/orders/:id/calls`, `GET …/calls`      | `update` / `read Order`       | call log                           |
| `GET /v1/orders/offers/mine`                    | `read Order`                  | my live offers                     |
| `POST /v1/orders/offers/:id/accept`, `/decline` | `update Order`                | offer response                     |
| `GET`/`PUT /v1/orders/workflow-settings`        | `read`/`update OrderWorkflow` | settings                           |
| `GET /v1/orders/team-board`                     | `read OrderWorkflow`          | supervisors' board                 |
| `POST /v1/orders/mark-printed`                  | `update Order`                | hub slips printed                  |

`GET /v1/orders` gains `queue` values `new`, `mine`, `verifying`, `callbacks`,
`confirmed_today`, `hub_print`, `hub_packing`, `hub_packed`, `hub_handed` and a `locationId`
filter; rows gain `assignedAt`, `calls`, `slipPrintedAt`, `hubStage`, `itemCount`.
`POST /v1/orders/:id/assign` gains the hand-over and reassign rules; `claim` names the winner.

New subjects: `Presence` (read, update; own) for org_admin, branch_manager, sales, warehouse,
marketing; `OrderWorkflow` (org_admin read and update; branch_manager read).

New error codes: `ORDER_HANDLED_BY_OTHER` (409), `ORDER_ASSIGN_NOT_ALLOWED` (403),
`ORDER_ASSIGNEE_INVALID` (400), `ORDER_OFFER_GONE` (409), `ORDER_CALL_INVALID` (400).

Events: `order.offered`, `order.offer_closed` (the person's room), `order.acted_over_owner`
(the owner's room), `order.released` (sales), `team.presence_changed` (org_admin,
branch_manager). Ids and display fields only.

Jobs: `orders.offer_dispatch` (org, one order), `orders.offer_expire` (org, `startAfter` the
offer's seconds), `orders.offer_sweep` (global, every minute: expire overdue offers and dispatch
waiting orders). The worker also dispatches on `order.created` and on `team.presence_changed`.
No external call anywhere.

### Console

- A lazy **work dock** in the shell: the heartbeat, a "Taking orders / Not taking orders"
  switch, the offer card (order number, area, items, cash to collect, a countdown, Accept and
  Decline; `aria-live`, keyboard reachable, no focus stealing) and toasts when someone acts on
  your order.
- Order detail: the ownership banner ("Handled by X since …" / "You are handling this order"),
  "Take this order", Release, Hand over, a confirm dialog before every action when you are not
  the owner, the calls panel and the hub stage.
- Desk queue tabs, the Hub page (`/orders/hub`), the Team board (`/orders/team`),
  Settings → Order workflow, the Staff page's desk profile dialog.
- Strings in a new `workflow` i18n section (English in both locale files until M43).
