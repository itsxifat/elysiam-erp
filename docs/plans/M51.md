## Scope

1. **Label to label** (D-930). At the packing station the parcel's label starts the lot: the
   scan that opens the order (its label, lot label, slip or courier code) starts lot 1's
   recording; every piece is scanned; the label scanned again stops the recording and closes
   the lot (the last lot's second scan finishes the order, which still needs every piece in).
   A piece scanned before the label is refused ("Scan the parcel label first"). Lots are packed
   in order (D-807): an order-level code means the lot being packed; another lot's label is
   refused, naming the one to scan. Close lot and Finish packing stay as buttons and stop the
   lot's recording too.
2. **Pause after 10 seconds** (D-931). No scan for the brand's idle time (10 s by default,
   3–60 s in Packaging → Packing settings) pauses the lot's video; the next scan resumes it.
   Every event at the table counts: a label scan, a piece, a refused piece, a piece taken out.
   The browser camera pauses and resumes its recorder; an IP camera's video is cut from the
   stretches the scans mark (each resumes two seconds before its scan, which its buffer holds),
   joined into one file.
3. **One video per lot** (D-932). `packing.recordings` keeps each lot's start, stop, source
   and idle time; every video part names its recording and lot. The order page and the station
   show the videos lot by lot under the one order.
4. **The return station** (D-934): Packaging → Returns. The returned parcel's label opens the
   order (on its way back, or partly delivered) and starts the recording; every piece that
   came back is scanned against what went out (a wrong product, wrong size or one too many is
   refused out loud); the label again stops it. Lots come back in any order, each its own
   video. Then the receipt: per line how many are damaged, the reason from the fixed list, a
   note, the damaged shelf when needed; Receive into stock books the return in the same
   transaction (`COURIER_API.receiveReturn`, the existing receipt, stock and journal).
5. **The screens, premium**: one scan-first workspace for packing and returns: a large live
   camera with REC / PAUSED / STOPPED and a countdown ring to the pause, a step rail (Label →
   Pieces → Label), lot tabs with each lot's state and video, the last scan's answer big with
   its sound, lines with photos and progress, and a timeline of the scans.

## Out of scope (follow-ups)

- A piece's condition chosen at the scan (the damaged split is made at the receipt).
- Lots of a return parcel told to the courier (no courier documents one).
- Re-recording a packing lot whose recording stopped at fifteen minutes (a return's bag reopens,
  D-937).
- What clears Goods in transit for a piece that never comes back (D-940, the owner's).

## Data model (migrations 0165, 0166)

- `packing.settings.idle_pause_ms` (3 000–60 000, default 10 000).
- `packing.sessions.kind` (`pack` | `return`), unique per order and kind; the session triggers
  learn the return: opened for an order on its way back or partly delivered, completed only with
  the order returned.
- `packing.scans.outcome` gains `label` (no SKU).
- `packing.recordings`: one per session, lot and take (a return's reopened bag); source (camera or this computer), whether a
  video is expected, idle time, started (the label scan), stopped (label, closed, finished,
  received, timed out). Set once, at most fifteen minutes (D-611). Its own triggers.
- `packing.videos.recording_id`, `lot_no`; the camera-part trigger accepts a stopped
  recording's camera.

## Domain (`packages/domain/src/packing/recording.ts`)

`recordingSpans`, `recordedMs`, `recordingPaused`, `judgePackLabelScan` (in order) and
`judgeReturnLabelScan` (any order, a done bag reopens), `isLabelReread`, `readOrderLabel` (which lot
a scanned code names for an order), `returnVideoExpiry`. Property tests: spans never overlap, lie inside the recording,
never pause within the idle time of an event.

## API

- `POST /v1/packing/sessions` (open) starts the lot when opened by a scanned code.
- `POST /v1/packing/sessions/:id/scans` judges label scans on the server: started, lot closed,
  finished, or refused with words; a piece before the label is `PACKING_LABEL_FIRST`.
- `POST /v1/packing/sessions/:id/videos` takes the part's `recordingId`.
- `/v1/packing/returns`: open (`code`), `GET :id`, `POST :id/scans`, `POST :id/take-out`,
  `POST :id/receive`, list.
- Settings take `idlePauseSeconds`.
