# M03: Ledger core

Phase 0 · Effort MAX · Depends on: M02b · Architecture §7, §27

## Scope, in my own words

A double-entry ledger the database itself refuses to corrupt. Every taka that
moves in this business ends up as a journal here, and the point of the
milestone is that there is exactly one way in, that it balances, and that
nothing can edit it afterwards.

This is the milestone the client's actual request rests on — "keep track of all
money". Every later module posts through `ledger.post()`: orders, COD
settlements, purchases, payroll, the till, investors, loans. If the guarantees
are wrong here they are wrong everywhere, and they are wrong silently.

**In scope**

- `packages/domain/src/money` — the branded `Poisha` type and the arithmetic.
- `formatMoney` in `packages/i18n`, ৳ with lakh/crore grouping.
- The `ledger` schema: accounts, journals, journal_lines, periods, and the four
  database guarantees (balance, immutability, period lock, idempotency).
- A chart-of-accounts template seeded per org, with a cash wallet per branch.
- `ledger.post(tx, draft)` and `reverse(journalId, reason)`.
- Period close, and reopen through the M02b approvals module.
- Reports: account ledger, trial balance, party balance, cash book, day book.
- Business dates in Asia/Dhaka (invariant 8).
- One posting rule end to end — cash in — to prove the pipeline.

**Out of scope**

- Every other posting rule. §7 lists thirty; each belongs to the milestone that
  owns its business event, and writing them now would mean inventing the events
  they post from. Cash in is the one that needs nothing but a wallet.
- Any UI. M05 brings the console shell; the reports here are service functions
  and tested as such.
- The outbox and realtime (M04).

## Data model

All tenant tables, all through `tenantTableSql()` (invariant 4, D-048).

### `ledger.accounts`

`id, org_id, code, name, class, subtype, branch_id?, party_type?, is_system, is_active`

- `class`: asset | liability | equity | income | expense.
- `subtype`: the enum from §7's table. It is what posting rules resolve against
  — a rule asks for `wallet_cash` at a branch, never for a code.
- `unique (org_id, code)`.
- `is_system` accounts cannot be deleted or have their subtype changed. The
  client can add accounts; they cannot remove the ones the posting rules need.
- A partial unique index on `(org_id, subtype)` for the subtypes that must be
  singular per org — `retained_earnings`, `cogs`, `sales_revenue` and the rest
  — because "which revenue account" must have one answer. Wallets and
  party-bearing accounts are exempt: there is one cash wallet per branch and
  one payable per supplier.

### `ledger.journals`

`id, org_id, business_date, source_type, source_id, idempotency_key, memo,
reverses_journal_id, created_by, created_at`

- `unique (org_id, idempotency_key)` — the idempotency guarantee.
- `business_date` is a `date`, not a timestamp: it is a Dhaka calendar day
  (invariant 8), and the whole reporting layer groups by it.

### `ledger.journal_lines`

`id, journal_id, org_id, account_id, branch_id?, party_type?, party_id?,
debit_poisha, credit_poisha, memo`

- `debit_poisha >= 0`, `credit_poisha >= 0`, and a CHECK that exactly one is
  non-zero. A line that is both or neither is not a line.
- Composite FK to `journals` carrying `org_id` (D-047).

### `ledger.periods`

`org_id, starts_on, ends_on, status (open|closed), closed_by, closed_at`

- One row per month per org. Closing is a status change; reopening needs a
  `period_reopen` approval, which M02b already reserves for super_admin.

### The four database guarantees

1. **Balance.** A deferred constraint trigger on `journal_lines`, checked at
   COMMIT: each journal has at least two lines and total debits equal total
   credits. Deferred because the lines are inserted one at a time and an
   immediate check would fail on the first one.
2. **Immutability.** REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE on both tables from `erp_app`, plus
   a trigger that raises — the same belt-and-braces M02b used on approvals,
   for the same reason: a grant can be handed back by a later migration
   without anyone mentioning the table.
3. **Period lock.** A BEFORE INSERT trigger on `journals`: a journal whose
   `business_date` falls in a closed period is rejected.
4. **Idempotency.** The unique index, plus `post()` returning the existing
   journal rather than raising.

## Domain functions

`packages/domain/src/money`:

- `type Poisha` — a branded bigint. Branded so a taka amount cannot be passed
  where poisha is expected; the ledger-auditor called the unbranded alias out
  and M03 is where it gets fixed.
- `add`, `sub`, `sum`, `mulQty` — total, no overflow surprises (bigint).
- `percentOf(amount, basisPoints)` — half away from zero, so a 2.5 poisha
  share rounds to 3 and −2.5 to −3. Basis points because percentages of
  percentages are where float creeps back in.
- `allocate(total, weights)` — largest remainder, so the parts sum to exactly
  the total. This is the one every invoice split and every reseller margin
  would have got subtly wrong.
- `parsePoisha` / `toPoisha` — the only way a string becomes money.

`packages/domain/src/posting/cash-in.ts` — one pure function from a business
event to a `JournalDraft`. Posting rules stay pure and tested (invariant 11);
the service turns a draft into rows.

`packages/domain/src/dates` — `businessDate(instant)` in Asia/Dhaka.

## Property and integration tests

- fast-check: random sequences of valid drafts leave the trial balance at zero
  and every journal balanced.
- `allocate` parts always sum exactly to the total, for random totals and
  weights including zeros and negatives.
- Rounding at .5 boundaries in both directions.
- Raw-SQL attempts against real Postgres: an unbalanced journal, an UPDATE, a
  DELETE, a journal in a closed period, a duplicate idempotency key.
- 23:30 UTC on the 1st is the 2nd in Dhaka.

## Assumptions to record

- **D-076**: the singular-subtype list. §7 does not say which subtypes must be
  unique per org; posting rules need a single answer for the non-party ones,
  so the list is enumerated in code with a test.
- **D-077**: periods are calendar months in Asia/Dhaka, generated on demand for
  the month a posting falls in. §7 says "closed period" without defining the
  grain.
