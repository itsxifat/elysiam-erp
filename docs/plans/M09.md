# M09: Suppliers and purchasing

Phase 1 · Effort MAX · Depends on: M07, M08 · Architecture §7, §8

> Written on 2026-09-30, after the build. M09 was built on 2026-09-26 without a
> plan file. This records the plan as executed.

## Scope

Purchase cost enters the system in exactly one place, the supply entry, and
supplier balances always tie to the ledger's party balances.

## Out of scope

- Stock movement mechanics and WAC arithmetic, which M07 owns.
- Wallet balances and cash entries, which M08 owns.
- Company loans from suppliers or anyone else (M26).

## Data model

Schema `supply`, migrations 0035 to 0038.

- `suppliers`, `supplier_contacts`, `supplier_notes`, `supplier_documents`.
- `supplies` and `supply_lines`: a header and lines. A draft may hold quantities
  with no prices (D-005).
- `returns` and `return_lines`, `supplier_payments`, `advance_applications`.
- 0038 adds the search index for the supplier list.

## Domain functions and what tests them

Posting rules in `packages/domain/src/posting`:

- `supply-received.ts`: Dr Inventory / Cr Supplier payable.
- `supplier-payment.ts`: payment, advance, and applying an advance to a supply.
- `supplier-return.ts`: stock leaves at WAC, the supplier is credited at the
  original cost, and the difference posts as purchase price variance.

Each has worked examples and a balance property in `supplier-payment.test.ts`
and `inventory-posting.test.ts`.

## API and screens

- 20 routes under `/v1/supply`: suppliers, contacts, notes, documents, drafts,
  entries, receive, payments, advance applications, returns, statement.
- Every response passes through `serializeFor()`. Warehouse and sales never
  receive a unit cost.
- Console: `/supply`, `/supply/entries`, `/supply/returns`, `/supply/payments`,
  `/supply/suppliers/$supplierId`.

## Decisions

D-005 (quantity-only drafts), D-095 (a supplier payment has no bank-charge
line), D-096 (a supply line may cost zero, a payment may not).

## Risks that mattered

- **A receipt that half-applies.** Movements, WAC and the journal are one
  transaction. Check 1 injects a failure in the journal step and asserts nothing
  is left behind.
- **A statement test comparing a query with itself.** The first audit found the
  check compared two copies of the same SQL. It now compares the statement with
  the ledger's party balance.
- **The module was never mounted.** `SupplyModule` was missing from `AppModule`,
  so every route answered 404 in production while its own suite passed. Found on
  2026-09-29 and fixed, with the routes added to the route matrix.
- **Four source files zero-filled by a crashed run.** Rebuilt on 2026-09-27.
