## What this is, in plain words

1. **A customer's voice message plays in the thread.** Meta's links to what customers send
   expire, so the worker downloads each voice message (and picture) and keeps it as a private file.
   The thread shows a player: play and pause, a waveform drawn from the audio that is also the
   progress bar, the position and length, and the speed (1×, 1.5×, 2×).
2. **Staff send voice messages.** A microphone button in the composer records (up to two
   minutes), plays it back, and sends or discards it. The server converts it with ffmpeg into what
   each channel takes (an Ogg/Opus voice note for WhatsApp, an M4A for Messenger and Instagram)
   and sends it through Meta; its delivery is recorded like a typed reply. The 24-hour window
   applies as it does to text.
3. **Calls ring in the console.** When a customer calls the brand on WhatsApp (or on Messenger,
   where Meta offers it), a full-width bar appears at the top of every screen of the person who has
   the conversation (or of everyone active on the messages desk when nobody has it), with the
   caller's name, the channel, Answer and Decline, and a ring made in the browser.
4. **Answer in the browser.** Answer picks the call up for you alone (a colleague who clicked too
   is told), opens the microphone and connects your browser to Meta over WebRTC; the bar then shows
   the call's clock, Mute and Hang up.
5. **Every call is in the conversation.** Answered (by whom, how long), declined (by whom) or
   missed. A missed call counts as unread and leaves a "call back" item, with the caller's
   WhatsApp number to ring, until someone marks it done.

## Scope

- Domain (`@elysium/domain/inbox`, pure, property-tested): a call's states and transitions
  (`callAfter`, `statusAfterTermination`), who rings (`ringTargets`), the call-back rule; each
  channel's voice format, what is kept, size ceilings and the retention rule.
- Files: an `inbox-media` purpose (private, read through `Conversation`, uploaded with
  `update Conversation`); audio types on the storage allow-list with their magic numbers; a
  produced file may have no owner; `FilesApi.storedBytes` for a module that checked its row.
- Server (`apps/server/src/modules/inbox`, new files): the call webhook parser, the Graph media
  and calls adapter (UNVERIFIED) with a fake, the transcoder, `InboxMediaService`,
  `InboxCallsService`, ten routes, four jobs, and the thread's enrichment. M42's webhook service
  calls them; its thread view shows them.
- Console: the voice player, kept pictures, the call entry, the recorder in the composer, the
  call bar in the shell (lazy), the WebRTC helpers and the ring; a `voice` strings section.
- Out of scope (follow-ups): business-initiated calls (calling a customer from the console), call
  recording and transcription, Instagram calls (no API), voicemail, video calls.

## Data model (schema `inbox`, `org_id` + RLS ENABLE and FORCE, composite FKs, never deleted)

- `inbox.media`: one row per kept attachment (message, position, kind, direction), its status
  (pending, stored, failed, expired), the file, type, size, duration and why it failed. A trigger
  fixes the message and place and lets only the keeping move.
- `inbox.calls`: one row per Meta call id: conversation, channel, status, WhatsApp's SDP offer
  (only while it can be answered), whom it rang, who answered or declined, the times, Meta's
  duration and reason, the call-back and its thread entry. A trigger keeps a finished call's
  outcome.
- Migrations `0151_m45_voice_calls_schema` (generated) and `0152_m45_voice_calls_policies`
  (custom), renumbered at integration after M44a's 0149 and 0150 (the SQL unchanged).

## API (M42's abilities: `read` / `update Conversation`)

- `GET /v1/inbox/media/:fileId`: a kept voice message or picture, as bytes.
- `POST /v1/inbox/conversations/:id/voice`: send a recorded voice message (`{id, fileId,
durationMs}`); someone else's conversation needs the acknowledgement.
- `GET /v1/inbox/calls/live`, `GET /v1/inbox/calls/:id`.
- `POST /v1/inbox/calls/:id/claim | answer | release | decline | hang-up | callback-done`.
- Events: `inbox.call_ringing` (to the people it rings), `inbox.call_changed`,
  `inbox.media_ready`.
- Jobs: `inbox.fetch_media`, `inbox.send_voice`, `inbox.call_timeout`, `inbox.media_retention`
  (nightly).

## Risks

- Every Meta adapter here is built from Meta's pages and has never spoken to Meta (UNVERIFIED).
- WhatsApp calling needs the number's calling switched on and a messaging limit of 2,000
  business-initiated conversations a day (D-789); until then no call webhook arrives.
- Messenger's Calling API is not offered to Pages in Bangladesh (D-789): built, likely idle.
- A browser behind a strict firewall may not reach Meta's relay (no TURN of ours, D-787).
