> the brand's ERP, conversations owned like orders, and a customer panel beside the thread. D-290
> had taken the Chatwoot inbox out of scope; this is a native one (D-730).

## What this is, in plain words

1. **Settings → Inbox**: the brand pastes its Meta app secret and verify token, its Facebook Page
   id and Page access token, its Instagram account id, and its WhatsApp phone number id and
   token. They are sealed at rest and only ever shown as "set" with a fingerprint. The screen
   shows the webhook address to paste into Meta.
2. **Messages come in** through one signed webhook. Each message opens a conversation for that
   person on that channel, or threads into the one they already have. A message Meta sends twice
   is recorded once; a body with a bad signature is refused.
3. **The inbox** (`/inbox`): every channel in one list, filtered by mine, unassigned, channel,
   stage and unread. The thread sits in the middle; the customer panel on the right. New
   messages arrive live.
4. **Ownership**: take a conversation, release it, hand it over. Anyone else who writes in it is
   first asked to acknowledge whose it is; the server refuses the write without that, audits it
   with it, and tells the owner.
5. **Replies** are queued in the database and sent by a job after the commit. Outside the 24-hour
   window the reply is refused with the reason (WhatsApp templates and Meta's message tags are out
   of scope). Delivery and read receipts update each message.
6. **The customer panel**: phone numbers found in the chat (saved in one click, which also
   matches the brand's customer with that number), the address with district and thana pickers,
   the matched customer's orders, delivered and returned counts and spend, dated notes, products
   they are interested in (live price, sizes in stock, photo), tags, and the stage (Visitor, Lead,
   Customer, Not qualified, Fraud). Customer is set automatically once they have ordered; Fraud
   shows red in the inbox and on the order desk for that phone.
7. **Create order** opens the order desk prefilled from the chat; the order records the
   conversation it came from.

## Scope

- Domain (`@elysium/domain/inbox`, pure, property-tested): phone extraction, the reply window,
  stage rules, delivery-status ordering.
- Server module `apps/server/src/modules/inbox`: schema `inbox`, the webhook (raw body,
  signature, verification), the processing job, the send job, the Meta gateway (Graph + fake),
  routes under `/v1/inbox`, realtime events, the order-created listener.
- Small additions elsewhere, through the owners' interfaces: `OrdersApi` (customer panel facts,
  the order's inbox origin, availability at the desk's location), `CatalogApi` (product cards
  without cost), the order source's `inboxConversationId`, contracts (subjects, errors, events),
  CORS (`x-acknowledge-owner`), nginx (`/webhooks/`).
- Console: `/inbox` (list, thread, panel), Settings → Inbox, the desk's prefill and fraud
  warning, the owner's notification.
- Out of scope: WhatsApp templates and Meta message tags (HUMAN_AGENT needs App Review),
  downloading media, merging two contacts, comments and story replies, auto-offer itself (M41),
  each person's work types (M41), syncing the Lead stage into `marketing.leads`.

## Data model (schema `inbox`, every table `org_id` + RLS ENABLE and FORCE, composite FKs)

- `settings` — one row per brand: the Meta app secret and the verify token, sealed, with
  fingerprints.
- `channels` — one per kind (`messenger`, `instagram`, `whatsapp`) per brand: the external id
  (Page id, Instagram account id, phone number id), enabled, the sealed access token (Instagram
  sends with the Page's).
- `contacts` — display name, stage (+ who/when), the saved phone, a link to `sales.customers`,
  the captured address (district, thana, area, line), tags.
- `identities` — a contact's PSID, IGSID or wa_id on one channel; unique per channel.
- `conversations` — channel, contact, identity, `assigned_to`, open/closed, last message at,
  last inbound at (the window), preview, unread count.
- `messages` — direction, text, attachment metadata, provider message id (unique per brand, the
  idempotency key), status (received, queued, sent, delivered, read, failed) with times and the
  error code.
- `notes` — append-only, author and time.
- `interests` — a product (and optionally a SKU) a contact asked about.
- `webhook_events` — the raw body first (sha256 unique per brand), processed by a job, the body
  cleared once processed.

## API

Public: `GET /webhooks/meta/:orgId` (verification), `POST /webhooks/meta/:orgId` (signed).

`read Conversation`: `GET /v1/inbox/conversations`, `GET .../:id`, `GET .../:id/panel`,
`GET .../:id/order-draft`, `GET /v1/inbox/team`, `GET /v1/inbox/phone-flags`.

`update Conversation`: `POST .../:id/take`, `/release`, `/hand-over`, `/close`, `/reopen`,
`/read`, `/messages`, `PUT .../:id/contact/phone`, `/contact/address`, `/contact/stage`,
`/contact/tags`, `POST .../:id/notes`, `POST .../:id/interests`,
`DELETE .../:id/interests/:interestId`.

`read`/`update InboxChannel`: `GET /v1/inbox/channels`, `PUT /v1/inbox/channels/:kind`,
`PUT /v1/inbox/meta-app`.

Every write on a conversation someone else owns needs `x-acknowledge-owner: 1`
(`INBOX_OWNER_ACK_REQUIRED` otherwise). Taking over, releasing or handing over someone else's
conversation also needs `update ConversationAssignment` (org_admin).

Events: `inbox.message_received`, `inbox.message_status`, `inbox.conversation_changed` (sales and
org_admin rooms plus the assignee's), `inbox.owner_overridden` (the owner's room). Ids only.

## Risks

- The adapters have never spoken to Meta (UNVERIFIED until real credentials are tested).
- Meta signs an escaped-unicode form of the body; both the raw bytes and that form are checked.
- Meta has no idempotency key on sends: a send whose result could not be recorded may repeat.
- The initial console bundle was at 249.7 of 250 KB and ends 27 bytes under it; the nav entry
  must stay tiny and the inbox adds no CSS rule of its own.
