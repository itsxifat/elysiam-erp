## What this is, in plain words

1. **Settings → Calls.** A supervisor adds the brand's Envo connection: its address, the API key
   and the webhook signing secret from Envo's Developers page (kept sealed; only a fingerprint is
   shown), presses Test (Envo's extensions and numbers come back), and pastes the webhook address
   into Envo.
2. **Extensions.** Each person who calls customers is given one extension. Their Call buttons ring
   it, and their phone inside the console uses it.
3. **Calling.** A Call button on the order page, its verification calls, the buyer page and the
   inbox's customer panel rings the person's own phone first, then the customer. A person with no
   extension is told so; a colleague's order asks for the go-ahead first (M41).
4. **The phone in the console.** Envo's web phone, inside the console: an incoming call rings with
   the caller's customer and their open orders; the ring-back of one's own Call is answered by
   itself; it says when it is offline or cannot start.
5. **Logs and recordings.** Envo's signed webhooks, and a sync every five minutes for any it
   missed, record each call against the customer and order with its person; the recording is
   downloaded into the ERP's files, plays where the call is shown for those allowed, and is
   deleted after the connection's retention (365 days unless changed).
6. **The Calls page.** Every call with filters, the recording player, the calls in progress (a
   supervisor may hang one up), and each extension's state.

## Acceptance checks (`docs/milestones.md`)

1. A connection is saved with its secrets sealed; the Test answers its extensions; a wrong key is
   refused with a clear reason. **Met** (`telephony.e2e.test.ts`, check 1).
2. An extension is assigned to a person; their Call rings their extension, then the customer; a
   person with no extension is told so. **Met** (check 2).
3. A signed `call.ended` is logged against the customer and order with its person; a bad
   signature or an old timestamp is refused; a repeated delivery is logged once. **Met** (check 3).
4. The recording is stored in the ERP's files and plays only for those who may hear it. **Met**
   (check 4, and the review's scope tests).
5. No call to Envo inside a transaction; no key, secret or full phone in a log. **Met for the
   originate and the audit log** (check 5); the other Envo calls are outside transactions by
   structure, and pino's own masking covers the logs (acceptance:test-1, a note).
