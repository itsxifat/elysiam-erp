### What this is, in plain words

1. **The inbox fills the window.** The list, the thread and the customer panel sit under the
   header and each scrolls on its own; the thread stays on the newest message (a "New messages"
   pill when you scrolled up to read); the reply box is always visible. On a phone or a narrow
   window the three take turns: the list, then the chat (with Back), then the customer.
2. **Names and photos.** Messenger and Instagram send only an id, so the list said "Unknown". A
   job now asks Meta's User Profile API after a person's first message, and again every three
   days while they keep writing (Meta's photo links expire); WhatsApp's name comes in its webhook.
   Faces are photos, or initials on a quiet tint.
3. **Alive.** New messages slide in, unread counts pop, the list glides when a chat jumps to the
   top, your status breathes when you are active, skeletons are shaped like what is coming, rows
   and buttons give a little when pressed, and each of our replies shows Sending, Sent ✓,
   Delivered ✓✓ and Read ✓✓ as it moves. Days are separated, consecutive bubbles grouped, times
   relative ("2 min ago"). Reduced motion turns all of it off.
4. **Sounds and alerts.** Tones made in the browser (no audio files): a soft chime for a new
   message, a brighter ring for an offer, a short glide when a reply is sent, two low notes on a
   failure. Each person mutes them or picks a volume in their account menu. While the tab is
   hidden a desktop alert names who wrote; permission is asked once, from a button. The tab's
   title counts unread messages. M41's offer card rings too: the urgent tone, a soft ring around
   the card, an amber countdown in its last quarter.
5. **Bangla.** Text with Bangla in it is marked as Bangla, so it is drawn in Hind Siliguri with
   no negative letter-spacing and a little more line height; English still costs nothing extra.
   The reply box takes the system's Bangla keyboard; Instagram's 1,000-byte limit is counted in
   bytes (a Bangla letter is three).
6. **Saved replies.** Settings → Inbox → Saved replies: a title, a shortcut, a body in any
   language and an optional category. In the reply box `/` opens them, filtered as you type;
   arrows choose, Enter or Tab inserts and fills `{name}`, `{order_number}`, `{total}` and
   `{phone}` from the chat (a placeholder with nothing to fill it stays selected). The five most
   used sit above the box.
7. **Keys and drafts.** j and k move between chats, a takes, e closes, / writes; a half-written
   reply waits in each conversation.

### What this is, in plain words

1. **The side card.** When a customer writes "আমার বুকের মাপ ৪২, এক্সএল সাইজের ২টা দিবেন", a card
   slides in at the top of the customer panel: "Size XL · Qty 2 · Chest 42 in → L (from the
   Panjabi size chart)", with Add to draft, Choose the product and Dismiss. It reads English,
   Bangla and Banglish, and Bangla digits; it changes as new messages arrive.
2. **A size from a measurement.** A chest, waist, length, height or weight is turned into a size
   through the product's own size chart (the website's, M33).
3. **The instant product picker.** Name, SKU, parent SKU or barcode, searched as the agent
   types; each product with its photo, price and a chip per size with the units in hand; driven
   from the keyboard (arrows, 1 to 9 for the quantity, Enter).
4. **The draft order.** Items, sizes, quantities and prices (editable in place), the delivery
   name, phone and address, the delivery charge from the zone, a discount, a promo code and an
   advance, with totals that move on every key press; Place order without leaving the chat, then
   a one-click order summary for the customer.

### How an order is placed (D-773, D-774)

The panel sends the order desk's own `POST /v1/orders` with the draft's `Idempotency-Key`:
channel by the chat's channel, `source.inboxConversationId`, the person placing it as its owner,
every line's price (the discount ceiling applies to a lower one), the delivery charge only when
typed (else the zone's), the promo code as a word. The order is pending; nothing is reserved until
the desk confirms it. An advance is then a claim (`POST /v1/orders/:id/advances`) for accounts to
verify; Place order waits for a transaction id M17 accepts, and the summary names the advance only
once it is recorded. The summary uses the saved order's figures when they differ from the draft's.
On a conversation someone else has, the panel asks first and records a note with the
acknowledgement (the owner is told); the order route itself has no conversation gate (follow-up in
D-774).
