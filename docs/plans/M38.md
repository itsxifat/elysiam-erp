## What this is, in plain words

Every piece in the warehouse carries a sticker with its own barcode. A new product is added
in one form (photos, sizes, colours, prices, website details) with the stock already in hand
and what each piece cost; the ERP then offers to print exactly one sticker per piece, on a
label roll or on A4 sticker sheets. A barcode scanner works anywhere in the ERP: scan a sticker
and the product opens, or, on a stock screen, the piece is added to the list. When a parcel is
packed, each item is scanned against the order, so a wrong size or a missing piece is caught
at the table, and the camera records the packing; the video is kept until seven days after the
parcel is delivered.

Built in two parts: **M38a** (products, stock in hand, stickers, scanner) first, because the
owner wants to start uploading products and stock now; **M38b** (scan-to-pack and the packing
video) after the website work.

## M38a: products, stock in hand, stickers, scanner

1. **The new-product form, complete** (D-601): everything the old website's product form had
   and more, in one form: name, category, description, status; sizes and colours as chips with
   a price per variant; photos (several at once, drag to reorder, choose the cover, optional
   crop to the shop's aspect); the website details from M33 (show on the website, featured, new
   arrival, gender, tags, size chart, material, care, SEO), collapsed by default; stock in hand
   per variant with the cost per piece. Editing a product reaches every field.
2. **Stock in hand at the start** (D-602): the stock the brand already holds when it starts on
   the ERP is entered per product, at a cost per piece (required), into a chosen location. It
   posts Dr Inventory, Cr Opening balance equity, one journal per entry with its own
   idempotency key (not M14a's single opening journal), and records `opening_balance` stock
   movements. This is allowed only while the brand's **opening stock window** is open (a
   setting an org admin closes once everything is counted in; closing it is audited and cannot
   be undone without an approval). After that, stock arrives only from suppliers, transfers,
   returns or approved adjustments. Every entry is audited and shown on the product's stock
   history.
3. **A barcode for every SKU** (D-603): each SKU gets an internal EAN-13 when it is created
   (prefix `2`, the range GS1 reserves for in-store use, then a per-brand sequence, then the
   check digit), stored in `catalog.skus.barcode` unless a supplier's barcode was given;
   existing SKUs are backfilled by a migration. EAN-13 rather than the SKU code in Code 128
   because a 19-character Code 128 symbol is too dense to scan reliably on a 38 mm sticker; the
   SKU code is printed in words under it. Lookup by barcode or SKU code everywhere.
4. **Stickers** (D-604): printed as a PDF sized for the medium:
   - thermal rolls: 38 × 25 mm, 40 × 30 mm and 50 × 25 mm, one sticker per page so the printer
     driver cuts correctly;
   - A4 sheets: the existing 3 × 8 layout and 4 × 10, with a "start at position" option so a
     half-used sheet is not wasted.
   - Content: the brand's short name, product name, size and colour, the price (on by default
     for clothing tags; the choice is remembered per person), the EAN-13 with its digits, and
     the SKU code.
   - Printed from: the end of the new-product form ("Print 24 stickers"), every stock receipt
     and stock-in-hand entry (one per piece received), the product page (choose variants and
     counts), and the stock list (select rows).
5. **The scanner everywhere** (D-605): USB and Bluetooth scanners type the code and press
   Enter. The console tells a scan from typing (fast keystrokes ending in Enter, outside a text
   field) and opens the product at that variant; on stock screens (receive, stock in hand,
   transfer, count, adjust) a scan adds or counts the piece. On a phone, "Scan with camera"
   uses the browser's barcode reader where the browser has one.
6. **Strict stock** (D-606): what is already true stays true (stock moves only through
   movements, never below zero, adjustments need a reason and approval over the threshold).
   Added: a stock count by scanning (scan every piece on a shelf; the count is the scans), and
   the product page shows each variant's barcode.

## M38b: scan-to-pack and the packing video

1. **Packing station** (`/orders/packing`): scan the order's slip (its QR code) or search for
   it; the screen lists the lines; each scan ticks one piece; a wrong item, a wrong size or one
   piece too many sounds an error and is refused; when every line is complete the order moves
   to `packed` through the state machine, and the courier label and invoice print. Who packed,
   when, and every scan are stored with the order.
2. **Packing video** (D-610): while the station is open on an order, the camera records
   (browser MediaRecorder, about 720p, about 1 Mbit/s, at most 15 minutes), with the order
   number, date and time drawn onto the picture; it uploads as a private file (purpose
   `packing-video`) linked to the order, and plays on the order page for whoever handles
   complaints. A recording that fails to upload is retried from the browser and flagged on the
   order.
3. **Kept until seven days after delivery** (D-611): a daily worker job deletes a packing video
   seven days after the order is delivered (or partly delivered); for an order that is returned
   or cancelled, seven days after that; every deletion audited. Storage on the server's disk or
   R2, sized in the plan's risk note.

## M38b build plan (2026-10-06)

Built on branch `m38b-pack-video` from `integration-wave2` at 00f9877 (Elysium's ERP is live:
migrations up to `0124` are frozen; M38b's come after). Decisions **D-610 to D-619**. The
decision numbers in the M38b section above (D-610 video, D-611 retention) were written before
the build; the rows in `decisions.md` are the ones that count.

### Scope

1. **Packing station** `/orders/packing` (D-610): find the order by scanning its slip (the QR
   or barcode carries the order number) or a courier code, or by typing; the lines with photo,
   product, size and colour, SKU, barcode and quantity; each scan of a sticker (EAN-13 or SKU
   code, M38a's scan field and camera) ticks one piece; an unknown code, a product not in the
   order, the right product in the wrong size or colour, and one piece too many are refused
   loudly (sound and colour) and logged; "Take one out" undoes a piece. Progress lives on the
   server (`packing.sessions`, `packing.scans`), so a reload resumes. "Finish packing" (or
   scanning the order slip again) moves the order to `packed` through the state machine
   (`OrdersApi.transition(…, 'pack')`, the same transition the Pack button and bulk pack
   use), exactly once; then the courier label and invoice print. Only `confirmed` orders, in
   the caller's `update Order` scope.
2. **Packing video** (D-611, D-613): while a session is open the browser records the chosen
   camera through a canvas that draws the order number, Dhaka date and time and the packer's
   name onto every frame (`captureStream` → `MediaRecorder`, about 720p, 800 kbit/s, VP8 WebM,
   MP4 where only that exists, no audio, at most 15 minutes). Chunks go to IndexedDB as they
   are recorded; on finish the recording uploads as a private `packing-video` file through the
   files module's ticket → PUT → finalize, then attaches to the session; a failed upload stays
   in IndexedDB and is retried; a reload mid-recording uploads what was recorded as part 1 and
   starts part 2. The order page plays each part (signed URL) and shows the scan log; a video
   that never arrives shows as missing.
3. **Retention** (D-612): `packingVideoExpiry` in `packages/domain/src/packing`: delivered or
   partly delivered, returned, cancelled or exchanged orders keep their video through the
   seventh Asia/Dhaka day after the day they reached that state, and lose it from the eighth;
   an order in flight never does. `packing.purge_videos` (daily 04:15 Dhaka) deletes each
   expired video in its own transaction: the session's video row marked deleted with the file
   id and reason, the file row removed, the audit entry written, and `files.remove_object`
   enqueued to delete the stored bytes outside the transaction (invariant 6's rule).
4. **Settings and the sessions list** (D-615, D-617): video on or off per brand (org_admin);
   the camera chosen per browser at the station; recent sessions on the station page (order,
   packer, started, duration, pieces, mismatches, video).

Out of scope: a "require the station to pack" switch (the Pack button and bulk pack stay; an
owner question), the Android app (stays read-only), server-side transcoding or thumbnails,
cleaning up abandoned `pending` video uploads (follow-up).

### Data model (migrations after 0124)

- `packing.settings` (one row per brand): `video_enabled` (default true), who and when.
- `packing.sessions`: one per order (`unique (org_id, order_id)`), `order_no` (orders never
  renumber), `status` open → completed only, who started and finished, `video_required`
  (the brand's setting when it opened). Composite FK to `sales.orders`.
- `packing.scans`: append-only (INSERT, SELECT): the client's `scan_id` (a retried scan counts
  once), the code as scanned, the SKU it named, the outcome (`accepted`, `removed`,
  `unknown_code`, `wrong_item`, `wrong_variant`, `over`), who and when.
- `packing.videos`: one row per recorded part (the browser's id, so registering is
  idempotent), the file once attached (composite FK to `files.objects`, unique), its length and
  size; deletion keeps the row with `deleted_at`, `deleted_reason`, `deleted_file_id`.
  Triggers keep identities fixed, a file attached once, a deleted row final, a session's
  completion final.
- RLS ENABLE and FORCE with the standard policy on all four; no analytics read.

### Domain (`packages/domain/src/packing`)

- `judgePackScan`, `judgePackRemoval`, `packProgress` (fast-check: an accepted scan never takes
  a SKU past its quantity; a SKU not in the order is never accepted; complete exactly when
  every SKU is at its quantity and nothing else was packed).
- `packingVideoExpiry`, `isPackingVideoExpired` (fast-check: never before the trigger + 7 days;
  always by the trigger + 9 days; never for an order in flight).

### API (module `packing`)

- `POST /v1/packing/sessions` `{ code } | { orderId }` (`update Order`): open or resume.
- `GET /v1/packing/sessions/:id` (`read Order`); `POST …/:id/scans` `{ scanId, code }`,
  `POST …/:id/removals` `{ scanId, skuId }`, `POST …/:id/complete` (`update Order`).
- `POST …/:id/videos` `{ videoId, startedAt, mime }`, `POST …/:id/videos/:videoId/file`
  `{ fileId, durationMs }` (`update Order`).
- `GET /v1/packing/sessions` recent, in scope (`read Order`); `GET /v1/packing/orders/:orderId`
  the order page's sessions, scans and videos (`read Order`);
  `GET /v1/packing/videos/:id/url` (`read PackingVideo`, the order in that scope).
- `GET /v1/packing/settings` (`update Order`), `PUT /v1/packing/settings`
  (`update PackingVideo`, org_admin).
- `OrdersApi`: `retentionFacts`, `visibleOrderIds`. `FilesApi`: `packingVideoFacts`,
  `signPrivateDownload`, `forget`; `files.remove_object` job.
- Files: `video/webm` and `video/mp4` (90 MiB, sniffed), accepted only for `packing-video`;
  each upload type's body limit is its own ceiling.
- Console headers: `Permissions-Policy: camera=(self)` and a `media-src` for the console.

- The state change: completion must go through the machine exactly once (session row lock,
  idempotent re-complete, the order's own lock).

  generic files route or the packing route.

Facts: the brand server (192.168.20.237) and the camera (192.168.20.111, RTSP on 554) share the

### What it is

1. **Settings** (D-668, D-669): Orders → Pack → Settings → IP cameras (org_admin): name, RTSP
   address without credentials (refused inside it), user name, a write-only password sealed
   with the brand's credentials key, on/off. `packing.cameras`, migrations `0134` (schema) and

2. **The station** (D-670): a recording source per browser (`erp.pack.source`): this
   computer's camera or an IP camera. Opening the order with an IP camera stores it on the
   session; the card shows Camera ready / offline; the browser recorder does not run.
3. **The window** (D-671): the first scan `judgePackScan` accepts sets `recording_from` = its
